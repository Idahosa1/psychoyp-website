PsychoYP, born Nicholas Ihua-Maduenyi is a Nigerian alternative hip hop rapper, singer, and songwriter. He is a member of the music collective Apex Village.

He came into the music scene with a critically acclaimed mixtape, YPSZN, which earned him a nomination at the SAMAs, and the Headies for his second fit album YPSZN2.

Dubbed "The Fresh Prince of Nigerian Rap", he is regarded as one of Nigeria's rap music pioneers in the alté music scene.
